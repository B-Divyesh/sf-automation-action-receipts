What verification means

A valid result means the recorded event chain matches the signed bundle. It does not establish real-world identity, prove that authorization was legitimate, confirm that an event actually occurred, or judge whether an action was correct. Do not use a receipt as the sole control for a high-risk operation.

Your responsibilities

  • Protect signing keys and rotate them if exposed.
  • Redact secrets and personal data before storage.
  • Set and enforce a suitable retention window.
  • Obtain permission before recording data about other people or systems.
